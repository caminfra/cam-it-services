## POSTING RHYTHM AFTER LAUNCH
- **2–3 posts/week** is plenty. Consistency beats volume.
- Rotate: 1 tip → 1 service highlight → 1 proof (a job you did, a review screenshot, a before/after).
- Every post ends with the phone number. Always.
- When you get your first review, screenshot it and post it. Social proof is your #1 marketing tool as a new business.

## GROWTH TIP
Join local Facebook groups — "Pearl MS Community," "Rankin County," neighborhood yard-sale groups — and just be helpful when tech questions come up. Don't spam. Answer a question well, and people will find your page. That's how the first customers actually come in.
